Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

In short
  • Your journal lives only on your phone, stored encrypted. We never upload it or store it anywhere else.
  • There are no accounts. We don't ask for your name or email.
  • Two features send information off your phone, and both are off until you turn them on: AI write-ups and anonymous diagnostics.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

Who we are

Nocturne is a dream journal app made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about this policy, contact us at 32nddegdesigns@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you put in your journal is stored only on your device: dreams, titles, symbols, moods, notes, voice recordings, synchronicities, the pre-sleep log, lesson progress, your birth details and chart, and your settings. The journal database is encrypted, and its key is kept in your phone’s secure keychain.

These features also work entirely on your phone and send nothing to us:

  • Birth chart and the sky each night. Planet positions are calculated on the device. The birthplace search uses a list of towns built into the app.
  • Morning reminder. A notification scheduled on the phone itself.
  • App lock. Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode are checked by your phone’s operating system. Nocturne never receives biometric data.
  • Recurring symbols, connections and patterns. Worked out on the device from your own entries.

Voice recording

When you speak a dream, the recording is saved on your phone. Nocturne asks your phone to turn speech into text on the device. On phones that can’t do that, your phone’s built-in speech service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android) processes the audio to return the text, under that company’s privacy policy. We never receive your recordings.

Exporting

If you use Export journal, Nocturne creates a text copy and hands it to your phone’s share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you.

What leaves your phone, and only if you turn it on

AI write-ups (off by default)

If you turn on AI write-ups, Nocturne uses Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, to polish your dream into a written account and to write a fuller astrology reading when you ask for one.

  • What is sent: for a write-up, the words of that dream (your answers to the guided questions, or the text of your voice transcript). For a reading, only your chart’s placements and that night’s planet positions, never your dream, name or birthplace.
  • How: over an encrypted connection to our server (hosted by Supabase), which passes it to Anthropic and returns the result to your phone.
  • What we keep: nothing you send. Our server doesn’t store or log the content of requests. To prevent abuse, each install of the app gets a random anonymous ID the first time it uses AI, and our server counts how many AI requests that ID makes each day. That count is all we store.
  • Anthropic: processes the request to generate the reply, under its privacy policy and commercial terms, which do not allow it to use the content to train its models.

Turn AI write-ups off at any time in Settings. Nocturne then uses its own on-phone write-up and reading.

Anonymous diagnostics (off by default)

If you turn on Share anonymous diagnostics, Nocturne sends two kinds of information that help us fix problems and improve the app. Neither includes anything you write.

  • Crash reports, through Sentry: the error and where in the app’s code it happened, your device model, operating system and app version. We turn off screenshots and activity trails, and our Sentry project doesn’t store IP addresses; it keeps only your country.
  • Feature use, through PostHog: anonymous events such as “a dream was kept by voice” or “a lesson was read”, with counts and yes/no details only (for example, how many symbols a dream had, never their names), plus when the app opens and closes, device model, operating system and app version. Each install has a random ID that isn’t linked to you. Location lookup is turned off and IP addresses are discarded.

Turning diagnostics off stops both immediately and resets the anonymous ID.

Deleting your data

  • Your journal: delete the Nocturne app, and everything stored on your phone is deleted with it. Export first if you want a copy.
  • Birth details: remove them any time in Settings › Birth chart; saved AI readings are removed with them.
  • The anonymous AI ID and diagnostics: these aren’t linked to you, so we can’t look them up by your name or email. Turning the features off stops any further data. Crash reports and usage events are kept for a limited time and then deleted by the services above.

Children

Nocturne isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Because Nocturne keeps your journal on your device and doesn’t collect identifying information, most of these rights are in your own hands through the app. For anything else, contact us at the address above.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Nocturne handles data, we’ll update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. Any new way of sending information off your phone will be off until you choose to turn it on.
